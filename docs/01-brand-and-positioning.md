# TOLVEY — Brand & Positioning

## 1. Brand lock

**TOLVEY** is the locked master brand for this repository.

This document records an operational brand decision, not a legal opinion or guarantee of registration.

## 2. Positioning

### Primary
**TOLVEY is a Product House Hub + Commerce House Hub.**

### Product House Hub
TOLVEY creates and manages its own product families: concepts, product specifications, packaging, versions, pricing, assets, and lifecycle.

### Commerce House Hub
TOLVEY owns the commercial operating layer: storefront, catalog, offers, checkout destinations, fulfillment/delivery, customer signals, analytics, and channel distribution.

## 3. What TOLVEY is not

TOLVEY is not initially:
- a generic marketplace;
- a multi-vendor platform;
- a social network;
- a payment processor;
- a logistics company;
- an agency selling arbitrary third-party services.

Third-party channels are distribution surfaces, not the definition of the business.

## 4. Brand architecture

TOLVEY
- TOLVEY Money
  - Money Kit
  - Budget Kit
  - Cashflow Kit
  - Savings Kit
- TOLVEY Business
- TOLVEY Creator
- TOLVEY Content
- future product families

Product names must remain subordinate to the TOLVEY master brand unless a later documented decision establishes a standalone brand.

## 5. Positioning evolution

The current positioning is intentionally broad enough to allow learning.

Do not over-specialize TOLVEY before evidence exists. Narrow the proposition only when customer demand, transaction data, or distribution evidence justifies it.
