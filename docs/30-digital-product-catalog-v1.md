# TOLVEY Digital Product Catalog V1

Status: Commercial catalog definition
Version: v1.0
Updated: 2026-09-30

## 1. Launch catalog

### MONEY
**Money Tracker** — income, expense, category and balance tracking.
**Budget Planner** — monthly budget and allocation workflow.
**Savings Tracker** — savings goals and progress.
**Money Kit** — integrated personal money management system.
**Money Complete System** — expanded planning, review and dashboard package.

### BUSINESS
**Business Tracker** — revenue, expenses, customers and orders.
**Profit Calculator** — revenue, cost, margin and profit calculations.
**Business Starter Kit** — core business tracking and monthly review.
**Business Kit** — broader operating toolkit.
**Business Complete System** — comprehensive small-business operating package.

### UMKM / SELLER
**HPP & Pricing Calculator** — cost, price, margin and markup.
**Order & Sales Tracker** — order and sales management.
**UMKM Starter Kit** — products, pricing, customers, orders and sales.
**UMKM Sales Kit** — catalog, offer, selling scripts and follow-up.
**UMKM Complete Kit** — sales + operations + finance.

### CREATOR
**Content Planner** — content calendar and production planning.
**Creator Idea Bank** — structured idea generation and capture.
**Creator Kit** — planning, production and performance workflow.
**Creator Monetization Kit** — content-to-offer and monetization workflow.
**Creator Business Bundle** — Creator + Business + Money components.

### FREELANCER
**Client Tracker** — leads, clients and status.
**Project Tracker** — project, deadline and delivery tracking.
**Freelancer Kit** — client + project + invoice + income workflow.
**Freelancer Complete System** — end-to-end solo service operation.

### SOLOPRENEUR
**Solopreneur Starter Kit** — offer, lead, sales and basic operations.
**Solopreneur Kit** — offer, sales, customer, delivery and finance.
**Solopreneur Complete System** — integrated operating package.

### AI WORK
**AI Work Starter** — practical prompt/workflow starter pack.
**AI Content Kit** — research, ideation, drafting and repurposing workflows.
**AI Sales Kit** — offer, outreach, follow-up and sales workflows.
**AI Business Kit** — structured AI workflows for business operations.
Products must emphasize workflows and outputs rather than raw prompt volume.

### PRODUCTIVITY
**Weekly Planner**
**Goal & Project Planner**
**Productivity Kit**
**Personal Review System**

## 2. Free / lead-generation products

Free products are acquisition assets, not substitutes for the paid catalog.

Initial candidates:
- Free Budget Tracker
- Free UMKM HPP Calculator
- Free Profit Calculator
- Free Content Planner
- Free Invoice Template
- Free Weekly Planner
- Free AI Work Starter

Each free product must point to one relevant paid product.

## 3. Bundle architecture

**Money + Business Bundle**
Money Kit + Business Kit.

**UMKM Complete Bundle**
UMKM Kit + Business Kit + Money components.

**Creator Business Bundle**
Creator Kit + Business Kit + Money components.

**Freelancer Complete Bundle**
Freelancer Kit + Money Kit + Business components.

**Solopreneur Bundle**
Money + Business + Sales + AI Work + Productivity.

Bundles should have a single customer job and should not simply be arbitrary file collections.

## 4. Price hypotheses

Initial test bands, not validated market prices:
- Free: Rp0
- Single: Rp15k–35k
- Starter Kit: Rp29k–59k
- Core Kit: Rp49k–99k
- Premium/Complete System: Rp99k–249k+
- Bundle: Rp99k–299k+

Prices must be tested by actual conversion, refund/support load, and perceived value.

## 5. Product packaging standard

Every paid product should ship with:
1. Product files
2. Quick Start
3. Version number
4. License
5. Usage instructions
6. Example/demo data where appropriate
7. Support instructions
8. Changelog
9. Product cover/thumbnail
10. Marketplace preview assets
11. Listing copy
12. Canonical product ID

## 6. Catalog rule

Do not launch the entire theoretical catalog at once.

Build a small representative set first, measure purchase behavior, then expand families based on evidence.
