# TOLVEY Money Kit — Product Definition

## Role

Money Kit is the first candidate product family for TOLVEY. It is a working hypothesis, not a guaranteed winning product.

## Problem territory

Practical personal/business money organization, visibility, planning, and repeatable financial routines.

## Initial product ladder

Free: checklist, simple tracker, educational starter asset.

Low-ticket: focused template/tool.

Core: Money Kit.

Bundle: broader money system.

Premium: implementation/automation layer where justified.

## Potential modules

- income tracker
- expense tracker
- cashflow view
- budget planner
- savings planner
- financial goals
- monthly review
- simple dashboard
- instructions
- reusable templates

## Validation requirement

Before expanding the kit, validate who buys, what problem triggers purchase, what asset they actually use, willingness to pay, preferred format, acquisition channel, and repeat/upsell potential.

Do not turn Money Kit into a giant bundle before evidence supports the added complexity.

## Success evidence

Advance a product when it demonstrates real behavior such as qualified demand, completed purchase, successful delivery/use, positive customer signal, repeat purchase/referral, or sustainable acquisition economics.

Internal usage alone is not market validation.
