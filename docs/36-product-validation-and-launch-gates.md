# TOLVEY Product Validation & Launch Gates

Status: Commercial operating specification
Version: v1.0
Updated: 2026-09-30

## Gate relationship — audit clarification 2026-10-01

These are **product evidence gates**, not engineering phases 0–8. They do not replace provider/customer-authorization/security/delivery/recovery/production release gates (docs/15/19). Money Kit remains hypothesis; choose one real candidate. Internal/test/controlled verification purchase proves mechanics, not independent demand. No product below is asserted to have passed these gates.

## Gate 0 — Definition
Product has a clear buyer, job, outcome, contents, format and license.

## Gate 1 — Build
Master asset package exists and passes internal quality review.

## Gate 2 — Test
Product is usable end-to-end by a fresh recipient and has truthful own-shop preview/terms/compatibility/support assets. Marketplace-specific creatives are needed only for an admitted external channel, not before every owned launch.

## Gate 3 — Listing
At least one approved purchase destination is live (own shop or eligible external commerce). Listing readiness includes real package/version/license/price/terms, ownership/private delivery/support and applicable release/policy gates. A listing alone is not a sale.

## Gate 4 — First Sale
At least one real independent customer payment is provider/channel-confirmed and fulfillment succeeds, with environment/source/version/amount/evidence recorded. A separately labeled controlled production verification transaction proves release mechanics, not this independent demand gate.

## Gate 5 — Validation
Evidence supports continued investment. Evidence may include repeated purchases, repeated demand, low support/refund burden, and a repeatable acquisition channel.

## Gate 6 — Expansion
Only validated or strongly evidenced products should receive substantial bundle, paid distribution or production expansion.

## Required evidence
Record dates, channel, offer, price, traffic/source, orders, refunds, fulfillment result and customer feedback.

## Language discipline
Implemented = built.
Listed = published.
Verified = tested/observed.
Validated = supported by commercial evidence.
Hypothesis = not yet commercially validated.
Blocked = dependency prevents progress.
