# TOLVEY Product Validation & Launch Gates

Status: Commercial operating specification
Version: v1.0
Updated: 2026-09-30

## Gate 0 — Definition
Product has a clear buyer, job, outcome, contents, format and license.

## Gate 1 — Build
Master asset package exists and passes internal quality review.

## Gate 2 — Test
Product is usable end-to-end and has marketplace-ready listing assets.

## Gate 3 — Listing
At least one compatible distribution channel is live.

## Gate 4 — First Sale
At least one real payment is confirmed and fulfillment succeeds.

## Gate 5 — Validation
Evidence supports continued investment. Evidence may include repeated purchases, repeated demand, low support/refund burden, and a repeatable acquisition channel.

## Gate 6 — Expansion
Only validated or strongly evidenced products should receive substantial bundle, paid distribution or production expansion.

## Required evidence
Record dates, channel, offer, price, traffic/source, orders, refunds, fulfillment result and customer feedback.

## Language discipline
Implemented = built.
Listed = published.
Verified = tested/observed.
Validated = supported by commercial evidence.
Hypothesis = not yet commercially validated.
Blocked = dependency prevents progress.
