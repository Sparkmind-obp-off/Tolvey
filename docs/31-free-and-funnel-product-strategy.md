# TOLVEY Free Product & Funnel Strategy

Status: Commercial acquisition architecture
Version: v1.0
Updated: 2026-09-30

## Purpose
Free products are useful acquisition assets that demonstrate product quality and route qualified users toward paid products.

## Funnel
Free Product → Single Product → Kit → Bundle → Complete System → Layer 2 Service when implementation is requested.

## Initial free catalog
- Free Budget Tracker → Money
- Free HPP Calculator → UMKM
- Free Profit Calculator → Business
- Free Content Planner → Creator
- Free Invoice Template → Freelancer
- Free Weekly Planner → Productivity
- Free AI Work Starter → AI Work

## Rules
A free product must solve a real small problem, be genuinely useful, have a relevant paid next step, and avoid deceptive claims.

## Measurement
Track visits, downloads, paid click-through, paid conversion, bundle attachment, service inquiry, repeat purchase, refunds and support load.

A download is a demand signal, not proof of willingness to pay.
