# TOLVEY — Validation & Observability

## Core hypothesis

TOLVEY can repeatedly convert real demand into profitable products through a shared product and commerce operating layer.

## Validation stages

Gate 0 — Demand: evidence of a real problem/request.

Gate 1 — Offer: a specific product promise and price.

Gate 2 — Transaction: at least one real purchase or equivalent strong buying signal.

Gate 3 — Fulfillment: customer successfully receives and can use the product.

Gate 4 — Repeatability: the same operating process works more than once.

Gate 5 — Distribution: a product can be distributed through multiple relevant channels.

Gate 6 — Economics: revenue, fees, fulfillment cost, refunds, and acquisition effort are understood.

## Minimum metrics

Product: views, product-page conversion, purchases, refund rate, delivery success, customer feedback.

Commerce: gross sales, net sales, orders, average order value, channel share, conversion rate.

Distribution: traffic by channel, content-to-click rate, click-to-purchase rate, channel fees, contribution after channel costs.

## Observability

Log product publication, price changes, order creation, payment state changes, fulfillment events, refunds, channel sync failures, webhook failures, and admin changes.

Use correlation IDs for cross-system events.

## Rule

No vanity metric should be treated as product-market evidence without a connection to buyer behavior.
