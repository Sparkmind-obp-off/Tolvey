# TOLVEY Layer 2 — Services Architecture

Status: Service catalog definition
Version: v1.0
Updated: 2026-09-30

## Purpose
Layer 2 sells implementation, customization and human-assisted execution. Layer 1 sells reusable digital products.

## First service workflow / acceptance — specification 2026-10-01

Offer one clearly scoped inquiry path first; inquiry is not an order/revenue. Flow: qualification → scope/dependencies/capacity → accepted proposal/terms → agreed payment milestones → delivery/revisions → acceptance/handoff → support. Record deliverable, input deadlines, timeline, price, revision limit, change control, cancellation/refund and support window. Do not allow instant-buy for undefined bespoke work.

Existing checkout fulfillment is DIGITAL/BLOCKED; SERVICE vocabulary is not a service execution implementation. Future typed scope snapshot/milestone/acceptance and secure client delivery require additive reviewed contracts. A future automation service is a possible customer offer, not a reason to automate TOLVEY before demand. Customization never changes reusable Layer 1 master files. See docs/19, docs/10 and docs/15.

## Service families (candidate offerings, not delivery proof)

### TOLVEY Presence
Website or landing-page implementation.

### TOLVEY Catalog
Digital product/service catalog implementation.

### TOLVEY Sales System
Offer, CTA, sales copy, inquiry handling and follow-up workflow.

### TOLVEY Sales System Custom
Tailored sales workflow, data structure, integrations and automation.

### TOLVEY Automation
Defined workflow automation across existing tools.

### TOLVEY Business System
Combined presence, catalog, sales and operating implementation.

### TOLVEY Custom
Scoped work outside standard packages.

## Layer 1 → Layer 2 triggers
- Customer wants a kit implemented.
- Customer needs branding/customization.
- Customer needs integrations.
- Customer needs operational automation.
- Customer has requirements beyond the standard product.

A Layer 1 purchase does not include Layer 2 work unless explicitly stated.

## Fulfillment requirements
Every service order defines scope, deliverables, dependencies, customer inputs, timeline, revisions, acceptance criteria, handoff and support window.

## Productization loop
Reusable patterns discovered during service delivery may become future Layer 1 products after deliberate productization and validation.
