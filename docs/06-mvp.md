# TOLVEY — MVP Scope

## MVP goal

Launch the smallest real Product House + Commerce House loop.

## Must have

1. TOLVEY landing/storefront
2. Canonical product catalog
3. First Money Kit offer
4. Product asset storage/versioning
5. Checkout destination
6. Digital delivery or fulfillment path
7. Basic order record
8. Basic channel attribution
9. Basic analytics
10. Product feedback capture
11. Admin/operator documentation

## Should have

- channel-ready product export
- UTM/source tracking
- coupon/offer support
- customer support workflow
- simple revenue dashboard
- product version history

## Later

- automated marketplace publishing
- multi-channel inventory synchronization
- advanced customer segmentation
- subscriptions
- affiliate management
- automated content generation
- channel performance intelligence

## Explicitly out of scope

- multi-vendor marketplace
- seller accounts
- escrow
- seller commission engine
- complex recommendation engine
- full accounting system
- custom payment processor

## MVP gate

The MVP is not complete because pages exist. It is complete when a real customer can discover, purchase, receive/use a TOLVEY product, and the operator can observe the transaction end-to-end.
